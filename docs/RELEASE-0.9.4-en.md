# Glossleaf 0.9.4

Margin notes now sit beside the document in a dedicated rail. In compact windows, a short writing slip opens below the page. Neither layout covers the source text. The slip saves after a brief pause; closing it restores the previous translation tab and scroll position. Escape closes it after saving; an empty new draft creates no note.

The page locator gives the current page and total room to breathe. Type a complete destination and press Return, or leave the field to jump. Escape cancels your input. Library and Memoir share the same sidebar width. Small fades and state transitions soften navigation and respect your system's reduced-motion setting.

Choose an application palette under **Settings → General → Paper & palette**: Paper & Ink, Celadon, Pine Soot, Sandalwood, Indigo, or Lotus Ash. Each works in light and dark themes. Saved highlights and original PDF colors are preserved; the brand mark and annotation palettes have their own settings.

PDF is currently the supported reading format. Other file types can remain linked attachments, with a clear “Attachment only” label. They no longer open mistakenly in the PDF reader. A reference with several attachment formats opens its available PDF first.

The app still does not provide OCR, EPUB / CAJ reading, embedded PDF annotation export, bidirectional Zotero sync, persistent undo history, or cloud collaboration. Complex inline translation remains experimental. Live model credentials and publisher access were not exercised in this release's isolated tests. A local mock response is not proof that a provider's current model IDs are available to a particular account.

This preview is built for Apple Silicon macOS and signed locally, without notarization. See the Chinese review and the accompanying verification record for the tested workflows, screenshots, and remaining validation limits.
